How your team uses it

  1. Ask. One line: the night, the subject, the tone.
  2. Get a draft. Caption, alt text, template and image direction.
  3. Fill the template. Drop in the photo and the text; the brand is locked.
  4. Approve and schedule. In Meta Business Suite, as you do today.

About 20 minutes from idea to scheduled post once the kit is in place.
